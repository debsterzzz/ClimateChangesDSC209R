How Our Planet Changed from 1992 to 2010

In less than one generation, the planet warmed faster than at any point in recorded history. Global averages make this story feel distant — but the real impact depends on where you live. This explorable explanation lets you uncover how temperature anomalies, sea levels, and climate-related disasters evolved over two decades.

How to Explore the Map

  • Use the year slider to move through time.
  • Press Play to animate the years automatically.
  • Switch between Temperature, Sea Level, and Disasters using the mode buttons.
  • Hover over any country to see exact values.
  • Use the country search to zoom into your home country and see its climate story.
  • The Global Snapshot Panel summarizes worldwide conditions for the selected year.

What Should You Take Away?

Together, the global map and the country search show that climate change is both a planetary story and a local one. Many countries move from mild anomalies to persistent warming, while climate-related disasters become more common over time. If your country’s lines trend upward, it means that the abstract idea of “global warming” is already visible in your own backyard.

The goal of this visualization is not just to show rising numbers, but to help you connect global trends to a place you know. When the global patterns and your country’s story line up, it becomes harder to dismiss climate change as something happening “somewhere else.”
