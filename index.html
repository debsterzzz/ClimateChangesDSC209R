How Our Planet Changed from 1992 to 2010

Climate change is a global phenomenon, but its impacts do not unfold evenly across the world. This explorable explanation lets you uncover how temperature anomalies, sea levels, and climate-related disasters evolved over two decades — revealing patterns that are invisible in traditional static charts.

How fast did the planet change in just 20 years?

How to Explore the Map

  • Use the year slider to move through time.
  • Press Play to animate the years automatically.
  • Switch between Temperature, Sea Level, and Disasters using the mode buttons.
  • Hover over any country to see exact values.
  • The Global Snapshot Panel summarizes worldwide conditions for the selected year.

Each mode reveals a different aspect of climate change — use them together to uncover the full picture.
